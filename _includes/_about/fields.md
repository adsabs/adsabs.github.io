    <p class="about-eyebrow">What is in the collection</p>
    <h2>Twenty research communities, one index</h2>
    <p class="about-lead">Earth science is everything we know about our planet, from earthquakes and volcanoes to oceans, climate, rainfall, soils and agriculture. SciX now holds 15 million Earth science records, searchable in the same place as the astronomy literature.</p>

    <ul class="about-fields">
      <li>
        <h3>Earth science</h3>
        <p>Geology, oceanography, meteorology, climate science, glaciology, hydrology, soil science, seismology and ecology.</p>
      </li>
      <li>
        <h3>Space science</h3>
        <p>Astrophysics, heliophysics and space weather, planetary science, astrobiology, and the instruments and missions behind them.</p>
      </li>
      <li>
        <h3>The sciences underneath</h3>
        <p>Physics, computer science and engineering, indexed because the Earth and space sciences are built on them.</p>
      </li>
      <li>
        <h3>Beyond papers</h3>
        <p>Datasets, software releases, observing proposals, grant records and gray literature, each linked to the work that uses it.</p>
      </li>
    </ul>

    <p>When ADS covered astronomy alone it was already the deepest index in its field. Extending that same depth to roughly twenty more research communities is what SciX set out to do. Astronomers kept the service they relied on, and geologists, oceanographers and climate scientists got one built to the same standard.</p>
  </div>
