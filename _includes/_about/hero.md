    <h1 class="about-title">About SciX</h1>
    <p class="about-lead">SciX, the Science Explorer, is a free search engine for the Earth and space sciences. It indexes more than 36 million papers, datasets, software releases and observing proposals, and it keeps track of how they are connected to each other.</p>
    <p>The Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory operates SciX under a NASA cooperative agreement. The people who build it have run the NASA Astrophysics Data System since 1993. Searching is free and needs no account.</p>
