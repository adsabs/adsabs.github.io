      <h2>Who builds it</h2>
      <p>About twenty people at the Center for Astrophysics | Harvard &amp; Smithsonian. The team holds four kinds of expertise that seldom sit together in one group: curators and subject librarians who read the literature and set the rules for it, data engineers who run the ingest pipelines, software engineers who keep a production service online for a global user base, and researchers who publish their own work on machine learning for science.</p>
      <p>That mix is why a small group can keep tens of millions of records accurate across fields as different as glaciology and high-energy astrophysics. Machine learning handles the sorting at scale, working from rules the curators wrote and checking back against their corrections.</p>
      <p>Most of us are researchers who use SciX for our own work, so we are among its heaviest users. Every message sent to the help address is answered by someone on this list.</p>
