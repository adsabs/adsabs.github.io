    <h2>How SciX is run</h2>
    <p>SciX is public infrastructure. The code, the models, the training data and the search interface are all open, and the work is funded to stay that way.</p>
    <ul class="about-tiles">
      <li class="about-tile">
        <span class="about-tile-icon" aria-hidden="true"><i class="fa fa-github"></i></span>
        <h3>Open source</h3>
        <p>Code, <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/scixblog/ads-models-and-datasets">models, and datasets</a> are public on <a href="https://github.com/adsabs">GitHub</a>.</p>
      </li>
      <li class="about-tile">
        <span class="about-tile-icon" aria-hidden="true"><i class="fa fa-share-alt"></i></span>
        <h3>FAIR</h3>
        <p>Records carry persistent identifiers and link out to data and software, following the <a href="https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/">FAIR principles</a>.</p>
      </li>
      <li class="about-tile">
        <span class="about-tile-icon" aria-hidden="true"><i class="fa fa-unlock"></i></span>
        <h3>Free</h3>
        <p>Search, browse, and export without an account. A free account is optional, for libraries and alerts.</p>
      </li>
      <li class="about-tile">
        <span class="about-tile-icon" aria-hidden="true"><i class="fa fa-code"></i></span>
        <h3>Open API</h3>
        <p>A documented <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/scixhelp/api-scix/">public API</a> for libraries, archives, and research workflows.</p>
      </li>
      <li class="about-tile">
        <span class="about-tile-icon" aria-hidden="true"><i class="fa fa-universal-access"></i></span>
        <h3>Accessible</h3>
        <p>Built for Section 508 and WCAG 2.x, and kept usable on a slow connection.</p>
      </li>
    </ul>
    <p class="about-closing">None of it requires a login. SciX is free, stays usable on a slow connection, and is kept to federal accessibility standards, so a researcher at an under-resourced institution has the same way in as one at a leading university. Most comparable indexes in the physical sciences sit behind a paywall.</p>
