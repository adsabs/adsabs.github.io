      <h2>Smithsonian Digital Innovation Award</h2>
      <p class="about-lead">In 2026 the Smithsonian Institution recognized the SciX team in the Digital Innovation category of the Smithsonian Excellence in Digital Awards. Twenty members of the team were named.</p>
      <p>The category recognizes a team that solves institutional challenges by applying new technologies, tools or processes. The citation credits SciX with carrying the Astrophysics Data System beyond astronomy into the Earth, environmental, planetary and space sciences, and with connecting papers to the datasets, software, observing proposals, grants, references and citations behind them instead of returning lists of publications.</p>
      <blockquote class="about-cite">
        SciX has extended a research infrastructure that astronomers have relied on for decades to many more scientific communities and provides a free, expertly curated and interconnected body of scientific knowledge that can serve as a trustworthy foundation for both human research and emerging AI-assisted scientific discovery.
        <footer>Smithsonian Excellence in Digital Awards 2026 citation</footer>
      </blockquote>
