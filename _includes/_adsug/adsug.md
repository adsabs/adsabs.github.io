The ADS Users Group (ADSUG) advises the ADS on the operations of the project, and recommends changes and improvements to both its services and procedures in order to maximize the scientific productivity of the community it serves. The ADSUG will advocate for the user community and provide suggestions regarding content curation, technical infrastructure, management, and priority setting.

Current members of the ADSUG are: 

- [John Wu](https://jwuphysics.github.io/) --- Chair; STScI
- [Laura Wolz](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/persons/laura.wolz) --- Vice Chair; Manchester U. 
- [Licia Verde](https://liciaverde.icc.ub.edu) --- Immediate Past Chair; UB-IEEC
- Matthew J. Graham --- Former Chair and [SciX Advisory Board](https://scixplorer.org/scixabout/advisory_board/) Member; Caltech
- Alex Atkins --- NASA JPL
- [Stéphanie Juneau](https://stephjuneau.github.io)   --- NOIRLab  
- [Sera Markoff](https://www.seramarkoff.com) --- Univ. Cambridge & Univ. Amsterdam
- [Brian Mason](https://crf.usno.navy.mil/wds/) --- USNO
- Ryan McGranaghan --- NASA JPL
- [Sam Teplitzky](https://samteplitzky.github.io) --- UC Berkeley Libraries
- [Paul Torrey](https://astronomy.as.virginia.edu/paul-torrey) --- UVa 
- [Louis Moresi](https://researchportalplus.anu.edu.au/en/persons/louis-moresi) --- [SciX Advisory Board](https://scixplorer.org/scixabout/advisory_board/) chair; ANU 

The next meeting of the ADSUG will take place on November 17--18, 2026, virtually and at the Center for Astrophysics \| Harvard & Smithsonian.  Please contact the chair if you would like to attend or have concerns that should be brought to the ADSUG. 

**[Agenda](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1g86KNWfFX10PUCwKjPuYF9b0dcSaXi-9DO0xIvjmIDw/edit?tab=t.0)**     

A list of [prior Users Group meeting presentations, reports, and membership]({{ include.root }}/meetings.html) is available.

Individuals interested in serving on the committee should register their interest by submitting their name for consideration to the PI and/or the ADSUG chair.

The ADSUG will summarize its deliberations in a written report to the PI, and its findings will be posted publicly on this website. Community input to the users group is welcome. Feel free to contact the ADSUG chair or any of its members individually.

For more information, please read [ADSUG's charter]({{ include.root }}/charter.html).
