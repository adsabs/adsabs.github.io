You can open [SciX](https://scixplorer.org) and search it without logging in. There is no subscription. A free account is optional, for people who want libraries and alerts. That is how the service is built, and that is what stayed true while the collection grew.

Search, browse, and export without an account. The corpus is about two and a half times larger than the ADS collection it grew out of, and existing accounts, libraries, and alerts carried over.
</div>
<div style="height: 15px;"></div>

The library now holds more than 36 million records across astrophysics, Earth science, heliophysics, planetary science, and physics. In FY25 it saw 11.6 million engaged sessions. From a paper you can move to the data, software, and grants behind it. The [models and datasets]{% if include.ads %}({{ site.baseurl }}/blog/ads-models-and-datasets){% else %}({{ site.baseurl }}/scixblog/ads-models-and-datasets){% endif %} that classify and link those records, including astroBERT and INDUS, are public on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/adsabs) and [GitHub](https://github.com/adsabs). The site is kept to Section 508 and WCAG 2.x, and it is meant to stay usable on a slow connection.

The Smithsonian Institution recognized that work. The SciX team at the Center for Astrophysics | Harvard & Smithsonian, operating the service from the Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory under a NASA cooperative agreement, received the 2026 Smithsonian Digital Innovation Award. It is the same team that has run the NASA Astrophysics Data System for 33 years.

The award was announced Smithsonian-wide on 24 September and will be presented on 19 November in Washington, DC. The citation and the nominated honorees are on the [awards page]{% if include.ads %}({{ site.baseurl }}/about/awards){% else %}({{ site.baseurl }}/scixabout/awards){% endif %}.
