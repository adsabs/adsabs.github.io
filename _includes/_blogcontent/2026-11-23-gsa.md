The SciX team had a ball in Denver, Colorado at this year’s <a href="https://connects.geosociety.org/?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=21894895623&gbraid=0AAAAA93p09i9SawsHdlqQuuYuxH5uhOQe&gclid=CjwKCAiA5Y_YBhBbEiwAK5Dl7TPnnQFT_VtWA42VKey9N67T3IHC4XPnOuOddqy_5wjskpEH8iuoJhoCcXUQAvD_BwE">GSA Connects conference last month. We came armed with our famous character stickers designed by <a href="https://scixplorer.org/scixabout/ambassador/team/Che.html">Yueyi Che</a>, a SciX Lead Ambassador and PhD student at UC Irvine, as well as our customisable button badges, and brand new to this conference, a SciX Selfie Frame and a box of cute research-related props, again designed by Yueyi! We left with the glow of many a great conversation, and promises to connect in the future.
   
<a href="../../{% if include.ads %}about{% else %}scixabout{% endif %}/team/team/jpomerantz.html">Jeffrey Pomerantz</a> (SciX Librarian) and <a href="../../{% if include.ads %}about{% else %}scixabout{% endif %}/team/team/skundu.html">Suze Kundu</a> (Community Engagement Consultant) had fascinating conversations about everything from atmospheric chemistry to zooarchaeology, with undergraduate students, early career researchers, publishers, and other research infrastructure groups such as EarthScope, while <a href="../../{% if include.ads %}about{% else %}scixabout{% endif %}/team/team/akelbert.html">Anna Kelbert</a> (SciX Project Scientist for Earth Science) gave a presentation.

The conference was a wonderful moment to reflect on the new ways in which people engage with our platform. SciX only hard launched out of its beta phase a year ago, and in that time we’ve gone from telling people about our platform to having people running to our booth to tell us how much we’ve supported their research, and that they’d love some merch to showcase their SciX Community pride! As a small team working on a big platform with an even broader impact, it means the world to us to have these interactions with the communities we are working to support.

We were also thrilled by how quickly some of our “subject pride” stickers got snapped up! “Geology Rocks” so hard that those stickers disappeared an hour and a half into the conference beginning. We took that feedback on board and from now on, not only will we be bringing more of those stickers, we’ve expanded our range to glorious glaciers, cheery cumulonimbus, laughing lava, and more! We’ll be premiering this new range at AGU in December, so if you’d like to get your hands on a sticker that taps into your love of research, come and chat with us there!
