Lisa Hees is the former Archivist & Assistant Curator at the Harvard Plate Stacks and is now a Research Associate for ADS and SciX where she is using her expertise in the history of Women Astronomical Computers and astronomical photographic plate metadata to bring forward the accomplishments of individuals who have been underrepresented in the history of science. She is developing digital resources for multidisciplinary audiences, enhancing digital asset metadata, and assessing and improving author identification schemes.

Lisa earned her MLIS from Simmons University with a concentration in archives management and a BA in Art History from the University of Texas at Arlington.
