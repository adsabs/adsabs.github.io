<div class="about-story">
<p>SciX Lead Ambassadors are community advocates working with the <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/scixabout/team/">SciX staff</a>. They help make research in astrophysics, planetary science, heliophysics, and Earth science easier to find. Ambassadors build a network in their cohort, promote SciX in their own communities, and contribute to the <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/scixblog/">SciX blog</a>.</p>

<p>The first cohort, 2024 to 2026, ran a workshop at the Center for Astrophysics | Harvard &amp; Smithsonian on 21 to 23 August 2024, along with monthly community calls and public events.</p>
