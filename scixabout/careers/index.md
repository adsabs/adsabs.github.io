### Who we are

The Science Explorer (SciX) is the expansion into the Earth sciences of the Astrophysics Data System (ADS), the primary digital library portal for researchers in astronomy, astrophysics, planetary science and heliophysics. You can read more about what SciX does on [our home page](https://scixplorer.org/home) and on the [About pages]({{ site.baseurl }}/scixabout/).

The SciX corpus holds more than 36 million bibliographic records, including refereed papers, preprints, gray literature, software and datasets. It grows by over ten thousand entries a week. Every astronomy paper ever published and nearly every physics and space science article is fully indexed. The Earth science collection launched in 2023 and is now complete. More than 50,000 scientists and librarians worldwide use SciX every day.

Alongside indexing and searching abstracts and full text, SciX tracks citations and usage to support discovery and evaluation. Records carry a web of links out to publisher sites, preprints, astronomical object information, planetary features, data catalogs and datasets held by external archives. SciX currently links to over 26 million records maintained by our collaborators.

In 2021 NASA asked us to extend our services to Earth science, planetary science, heliophysics and NASA-funded research in the biological and physical sciences. We changed our name to SciX to reflect that broader mission. The expansion lets us work with every NASA science division and bring Earth and space scientists into the same index.

SciX builds and maintains the services that make scholarly literature in these fields discoverable. We follow the open practices NASA has long championed: our software is released under an open source license, and our data holdings are available through an open API.

The platform runs on a modern stack: a custom build of the open source search engine Solr, a React front end, a high performance distributed brokerage system, and cloud-hosted services on Kubernetes in Amazon Web Services. The primary programming languages are Python, Java and JavaScript/TypeScript.

### What we offer

NASA funds SciX through a cooperative agreement. We are hosted by the Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory at the Center for Astrophysics | Harvard & Smithsonian (CfA) in Cambridge, Massachusetts, one of the largest and most diverse astrophysical institutions in the world and home to a growing Earth science community. Joining us means supporting the discovery and dissemination of scientific results for the worldwide research community.

Some of the perks of the job:

  * Work with a creative, multi-talented team in a first-class academic environment
  * Entry salary typically within the 82-152K range (with yearly revisions) for grades 11/12/13 in the Boston area (see the current [location-dependent pay scales](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/salaries-wages/salary-tables/))
  * [Generous benefits](https://pweb.cfa.harvard.edu/people/smithsonian-astrophysical-observatory-human-resources/sao-trust-fund-employee-benefits), flexible work hours, and telecommuting and hybrid options local to the Cambridge, MA area
  * Access to the scientific community and events at the CfA and Harvard University
  * Engage with users at community meetings and present your work at conferences in astronomy and information science
