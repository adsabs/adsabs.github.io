<p class="about-lead">About twenty people build and curate SciX at the Center for Astrophysics | Harvard &amp; Smithsonian.</p>

<p>Between them they cover four kinds of work that seldom sit in one group: curation and subject librarianship, data pipelines, production software engineering, and research on machine learning for science. That range is what lets a team this size keep more than 36 million records accurate across fields as different as glaciology and high-energy astrophysics.</p>

<p>Most of us are working researchers who use SciX for our own projects. Write to <a href="mailto:help@scixplorer.org">help@scixplorer.org</a> and someone on this page will answer.</p>
