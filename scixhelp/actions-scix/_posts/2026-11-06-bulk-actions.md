The **Bulk Actions** menu, at the top right of the search results page next to **Explore**, acts on many results at once. Choose **All** to use every result, or **Selected** to use only the results you have checked.

* **Export**: export the results in BibTeX, AASTeX, EndNote or RIS, or choose **Other Formats** for the full list. See [Export Results](../actions-scix/export).
* **Author Affiliations**: build a report of the authors of the results and their affiliations, for example for a grant application's collaborator list. See [Author Affiliation](../actions-scix/author-affiliation).

To copy a single citation instead, see [Copy Citation](../actions-scix/copy-citation).
